3. Description of services

CarVault allows you to record, collect and display vehicle sightings and offers
gamification features (including Car Quartet, collections and statistics). CarVault Premium unlocks additional features
for a fee; the current scope of features is shown in the app.

Vault Coins are an in-game currency within the app. You earn them by playing (e.g. levels,
achievements, Car Quartet, daily tasks) or buy them in packs (Section 4).
Earned and purchased coins form a single shared balance. With coins you buy tuning parts in the
shop, which improve the values of a card in Car Quartet; a part is used up when you reveal it
in an online match. Coins and tuning parts have no monetary value: they cannot be exchanged for
money, cannot be paid out and cannot be transferred to another account. What a purchase
contains is shown to you before you buy; there are no random contents.

For game balance, we may adjust the range of the shop, the coin prices of the tuning parts and the effect of
individual parts. This also applies to parts that you already own, because their effect is only calculated when
they are used. We never reduce your coin balance in the process. For paid content, such an adjustment is a change
for a valid reason within the meaning of the following paragraph.

For paid services, we change the scope of features only for a valid reason (e.g. security, a change in the law,
further development) and at no additional cost to you. If a change affects your access to a paid service or
its usability by more than a minor degree, we will inform you in advance in the app, and you may terminate the
contract for that service free of charge within 30 days of the information or, if later, of the change
(§ 327r German Civil Code, BGB). For the free basic use, the scope of features and the availability of

16. Crews, weekly event and bolts

Crews are open to you from the age of 16. In the weekly event, the app credits you with the in-game currency “bolts”
for the performance of your crew. Bolts are only available in this way: you cannot buy them, exchange them for money or
Vault Coins, have them paid out or transfer them to another account. They have no monetary value and do not expire;
they lapse when your account is deleted.

In the crew shop, you exchange bolts for content: boosts for your whole crew (24 hours each; at most one of each type
at a time and one per calendar week), a card frame for yourself and – as founder – a crew colour. A boost belongs to
the crew and keeps running if you leave it; your display name is shown as the sponsor. The card frame is visible to
you and to others only for as long as you are a member of the crew for which you obtained it. If a crew is dissolved,
its boosts, its colour and the card frames obtained for it lapse; bolts that have been spent are not refunded.

We may change the prices, effects and range of the crew shop for reasons of game balance; a boost that is already
running remains unaffected. We may withdraw bolts that were obtained through manipulation or by exploiting errors.
