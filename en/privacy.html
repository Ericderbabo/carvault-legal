4. Sightings: photos and location

When you photograph a vehicle, the photo is stored on our servers and assigned to your account.
Before uploading, we automatically remove any EXIF metadata contained in it (including
device information and GPS coordinates embedded in the image data itself), so that this
information is not disseminated in the image itself in addition to the separately recorded
location.

A photo taken in a public space almost always shows more than just a car — above all
official licence plates, and occasionally persons in the image. A licence plate can be used to identify
the keeper of a vehicle; it is therefore personal data, even though we ourselves can neither make
nor are permitted to make this assignment.

For this reason, the app obscures licence plates before a photo leaves your device: before every
upload and before every AI recognition, it searches for licence plates directly on your device and
permanently covers the places it has found in the image. You see what it has found before
saving and can add to it or correct it yourself. Only the version edited in this way reaches our servers and the
recognition service (Section 6). The unaltered original remains solely on your device. For text recognition, the app uses Google’s ML Kit. The
recognition itself runs on your device, but according to Google the software package may transmit technical
usage and diagnostic data to Google.

This detection works automatically and is not error-free — a heavily rotated, obscured
or poorly lit licence plate may escape it. The app does not obscure persons in the image. It therefore does not replace your own
consideration: do not upload photos in which persons can be recognised who would not
agree to this.

Sighting photos are not public. They are assigned to your account and can never be retrieved by
strangers — not even in Car Quartet, where only vehicle data from our catalogue
is transmitted and never photos.
The only exception, which you control yourself: the title photo of your spots for your confirmed
friends (Section 22). We
do not search image content and do not read out licence plates.

The legal basis for processing image content relating to third parties is our
legitimate interest in operating a collecting and documentation app as well as your interest in
documenting your own observations (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We have weighed this interest
against the rights of the data subjects; the obscuring of licence plates, the fact that photos are not
public and the refraining from any analysis of image content are the result of
this balancing.

We cannot inform affected third parties individually about the storage: we do not know them
and are not permitted to identify them — providing information would therefore be impossible within the meaning of Art. 14
(5)(b) GDPR. This section is the public
information provided in its place. Anyone who suspects they are depicted in a photo stored in CarVault can
contact the address stated in Section 1 at any time; we will review the case and delete
the photo concerned, insofar as it can be assigned.

The location of a sighting is optional: we query your GPS position live; if you refuse or
deactivate this, the sighting is saved without a location — this never blocks use.
The legal basis for the collection of location data is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which
you can withdraw at any time via the permissions of your operating system.

From the coordinates, we derive on our own server the name of the nearest place, which is displayed with the
sighting. For this purpose, we use a place dataset stored on the server (GeoNames); coordinates are not
transmitted to anyone in the process.

6. AI-assisted vehicle recognition

To automatically recognise which vehicle is shown in a photo, the image is transmitted to
Google Ireland Limited (Gemini API) and analysed there by an AI model.

Only the photo itself is transmitted, reduced to a maximum of 1024 pixels
edge length. The location of your sighting is not sent along, and EXIF metadata
(including GPS coordinates) is removed by the app on your device before a photo is
even uploaded. If we recognise the same photo a second time, a
cache applies — it then does not leave our server a second time.

We use the paid tier of the Gemini API. There, Google contractually does not use the transmitted content to improve its own products or to train AI models
and stores it only for a limited time in order to detect abuse. Processing takes place as
commissioned processing; a transfer to the USA is possible in this context (see Section 13).

Before a sighting is saved, we additionally check whether the photo shows a real vehicle
and not, for example, a screen or a printout. For this purpose, the same photo is transmitted in a separate
call to the same service; the same conditions apply as above. We do not store the result
of this check. If the photo evidently does not show a genuine picture of a vehicle,
the sighting is not created. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in fair
play for all users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The result is always only a suggestion — the app never assigns a vehicle automatically; you
always confirm or correct the recognition yourself. For this reason, there is no
automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect within the meaning of Art. 22
GDPR. You can find further information on the use of AI in Section 17.

We store the brand that the recognition service suggests as the most likely one with your sighting. On this
basis, we decide whether the sighting can be played in Car Quartet: if you assign it an engine variant of a
different brand that the recognition did not suggest, or if no recognition took place, it cannot be played; you
can still collect it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in fair play (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

For cost control and quality assurance, we log for each recognition request a
checksum of the image as well as the result (no plain-text personal reference other than your
user ID). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the recognition function) and
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protection against abuse and costs).

21. Error diagnosis and crash reports

If the app crashes or an error occurs that we did not anticipate, we send a
technical error report to Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry). Without such reports, we would not
learn of a crash on your device.

A report contains exclusively technical information:

  • type of error, error message and the place in the program code where it occurred
  • device model, operating system version, app version and the program libraries used
  • the last steps before the error, such as which screen was opened and which button

was last tapped

  • whether an app session ended with a crash

Not included are: your name, your email address, an identifier of your account, your photos,
your location data and the content of your network requests. Your IP address also does not become part of
the report: the app does not transmit it, and Sentry does not store it with the report. An
error report is therefore not assigned to any particular person; a link to you can at most arise indirectly
from the description of how the error occurred.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a functional and stable app

(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

  • Storage location: data centres within the European Union
  • Retention period: 90 days, after which the reports are automatically deleted
  • You can object to this processing at any time pursuant to Art. 21 GDPR: Settings →

Privacy → switch off “Send crash reports”. The objection applies from the next launch
of the app; after that, no report is created or transmitted

22. Friends

friend code, a link or a QR code, and a friendship is only established when the
other person accepts the request; there is no search by name. The only exception without a code: after a

hours (not after rounds with friends); the recipient cannot tell from the request that it comes from a match. If you redeem
another person’s invitation code (Section 10), we automatically send them, in your name,

Your confirmed friends see your display name, your level and how many spots you have per

Title photos for friends: If the setting “Show my title photos to friends” is switched on
(default), your confirmed friends see the title photo of your spots, cropped

the places found (Section 4). We share older photos without this pass only

friends, even if the setting is switched on.

Friends can report a title photo, for example if a person is recognisable or a licence plate
is legible on it. From three reports onwards, we hide the photo from all friends until we have reviewed it;
after that, we either withdraw the release permanently or lift the hiding. You do not learn who reported your photo.
We delete resolved reports after 180 days. Anyone who suspects they are depicted on such a
photo can additionally contact the address stated in Section 1;
a justified objection (Art. 21 GDPR) also ends the release to friends.

  • Legal basis for friendships and the display of your title photos: performance of the

limit of two per day.

  • Legal basis for push notifications: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and

24. Crews

Crews are small groups with a weekly and an overall ranking. You can found crews or join them
if you are at least 16 years old. Every crew can be found via a search for its name;
you can join only by a request that the founder accepts; there is neither a code
nor a link. A crew has up to 5 members, with the founder’s Premium up to 25, and you
can only be in one crew.

Visible to all accounts aged 16 and over are the name, symbol, number of members and total points of a crew,
in the search and in the crew ranking. The members of a crew see each other’s display name and experience points (XP)
and, for each member, level, the number of their finds of the tiers Rare, Epic and Legendary, their
online wins and losses in Car Quartet (excluding matches with friends) as well as their joining date –
even if they are not friends. They do not see photos, places or vehicle names in the process. In addition,
there is a statistic of the crew (Car Quartet record since joining, collection, the three best
members). The points of a crew are the experience points (XP) of its members; weekly XP
count from joining, total XP retroactively. For this purpose, we store the
membership with joining date and the requests.

Reporting crew names: Every user aged 16 or over can report the name of another crew as inappropriate. The
report is anonymous to the founder. For this purpose, we store the crew, your account as the reporter and the
reported name. For you yourself, the name is subsequently displayed as *. If several different
users have reported the same name, the founder is informed of this in the app, without learning who
reported it; with even more reports, the founder must change the name and the name is hidden from all others
in the meantime. We delete the reports as soon as the crew name is changed, the crew is deleted or your
account is deleted. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a platform without
inappropriate crew names (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We check crew names with the same name rule as display names (Section 8). You can also report
conspicuous names to the address in Section 1. If the founder leaves the crew, the
longest-serving member becomes founder; if you delete your account as a founder, the longest-serving member takes over the
crew, and it is deleted if nobody remains.

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention period: until you leave the crew, are removed from it or delete your account; requests

until a decision is made

25. Date of birth

At first launch, we ask for your date of birth. We use it only to comply with age limits: the minimum age of 13
years, not collecting location for accounts under 16 years of age (Section 16), crews and the purchase of coin
packs from the age of 16, and individual default settings for accounts under 16 years of age. The information is
not verified and can be changed in the settings.

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use to the extent permitted in each case (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and

our legitimate interest in the protection of minors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

  • Retention period: until your account is deleted; the date of birth is part of your data export

26. Garage and own vehicles

In the garage, you add your own vehicles. For this purpose, we store, for each slot, the vehicle, up to 30
photos, the values you enter yourself for the vehicle and its tuning settings. We treat the photos like
sighting photos (Section 4): licence plates are searched for before upload and, where found, obscured, EXIF
metadata is removed, and the photos are not public. You can use the AI vehicle recognition to add a vehicle;
the same rules apply as in Section 6. Other users do not see your own vehicles, and neither do your friends
(Section 22).

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention period: until you delete the vehicle or photo or delete your account; when Premium ends, slots 2

and 3 are emptied together with the vehicle, photos and tuning, while purchased slots remain

27. Daily tasks and weekly streak

For the weekly streak, we store on which calendar days you opened the app – only the date, no time of day. We
calculate daily tasks and their rewards from your sightings and matches; all that is stored is that you
completed a task, together with the reward (XP and coins).

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention period: we delete the days on which you opened the app after 14 days; completed daily tasks and

rewards remain until you delete your account

28. Right to object

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – this
concerns in particular image content relating to third parties, the authenticity check, reports, feedback,
technical logs, crash reports and the map display – you can object at any time on grounds relating to your
particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal message to support@carvaultapp.de is sufficient. You can also
object to crash reports in the app (Section 21).

29. Server logs

When the app calls our servers, we log, for technical reasons, the time, the address called, the request method,
the response status, the duration and, for signed-in requests, your user identifier; the upstream web server may
additionally log your IP address. We use these logs for troubleshooting, to secure operation and to defend
against abuse.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
  • Retention period: 14 days

30. Profile picture

In your profile, you can upload a profile picture. Before uploading, the app reduces the image in size and
removes the EXIF metadata as for sighting photos (Section 4); it also searches for licence plates on your device
and, where found, obscures them. You see what it found before uploading and can add to it yourself. Only you yourself see the picture; friends and members of your crew see it only if you share it (Section 33), fellow players never. You can
replace it with a new one at any time; the app currently does not offer a way to remove it without a replacement.

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention period: the current profile picture until you replace it with a new one or delete your account; a

replaced profile picture is deleted as soon as you upload the new one

31. Crew weekly event and bolts

Each week, the app gives every crew with at least two members the same seven tasks. Counting uses data that the
app already stores: sightings with the time of their photo, online Quartet matches against random opponents,
completed daily tasks and days with a check-in. No additional data is collected for this.

For each crew, we store the weekly progress (points and status per task) and, after the end of the week, an
archive with points and rank. Neither has any reference to individual persons. Other crews therefore see
only figures of the crew (points, rank), not who contributed how much; only the members of the same crew see the contribution of each member in the current week (Section 33).

For members with a contribution of their own, the server credits the in-game currency “bolts” to your account
after the end of the week. For each booking, we store the type, the week reference, the amount and the time.
Bolts have no monetary value, cannot be bought or paid out, and go only to members who are still in the crew
when the week is closed.

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention period: weekly progress and archive for as long as the crew exists; the bookings of your bolts until

your account is deleted – they are then deleted; they are included in the data export

32. Crew shop

In the crew shop, you exchange bolts (Section 31) for boosts for your crew and for cosmetic items. For this, we store
the spending as a booking of your bolts (type, item, purchase identifier, amount, time), for a boost the crew, the
type, the time window and you as the sponsor, for a card frame you and the crew, and for a crew colour the colour of
the crew.

The following is visible: to all members of your crew, which boost is running, until when and who treated the crew to
it (your display name); your card frame in the member list of your crew and, together with the tag of your crew (the tag is omitted while the name of your crew is reported), to
fellow players in online Quartet; the title “Crew veteran” in the member list. We do not store the title separately;
it results from your bolt bookings. If a boost of your crew is running at the start of an online match, we take it into account
when settling the match.

After the end of the week, we send you a push notification with the rank of your crew, provided that you allow push
notifications about friend activity (Section 23); it counts towards the daily maximum of these notifications.

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Retention period: boosts, card frames and crew colour for as long as the crew exists; if you delete your account, we

remove you as the sponsor (the boost stays with the crew) and delete your card frames; for the bookings of your bolts,
see Section 31; all of this is included in the data export

33. Profile picture for friends and crew, vice and hints in the crew

Sharing your profile picture: If you switch on “Show profile picture to friends and crew” (default: off), your
confirmed friends and the members of your crew see your profile picture – cropped to a square and reduced in size;
others never see the part you cropped away (the original stays stored, Section 30). Fellow players in online Quartet do not see it. Accounts under
16 never share their profile picture and do not see the profile pictures of others. If you switch the setting off, a
friendship ends or you leave the crew, the picture is immediately no longer visible to those concerned. Anyone who may
see your profile picture can report it; for this purpose, we store the reporting person, the reason and the time.
Whoever reported it no longer sees the picture afterwards. From three reports by different persons, we hide the
picture until we have reviewed it; afterwards we lift the hiding or permanently withdraw the sharing of this picture.

Friend request from the crew: Members of the same crew can send each other a friend request; Section 22 applies
(accounts under 16 can only accept).

Vice: The founder can appoint members as vice and withdraw the role again; a vice can accept or decline requests to
join and remove members without a role. We store the role with the membership. If the founder leaves the crew or
deletes their account, in deviation from Section 24 the longest-serving vice takes over first, otherwise the
longest-serving member. If the founder has not been active for 30 days, the app offers the longest-serving vice to take
over the crew; the founder then remains a member.

Activity and contribution: For each account, we store the last day on which the app reached our server – a single
date that is overwritten with every use. From this, the members of your crew see for how many days you have not been
active (from three days on), and how much you have contributed to the weekly event (Section 31) in the current week. A
member of your crew can “nudge” you once per week; we then send you a push notification if “Push for friend activity”
is switched on (Section 23), at most two notifications per day together with those from Section 23 (not in addition) and not between 9 pm and 8 am. For this purpose,
we store the nudging and the nudged person, the crew and the week and delete the entry after 15 days.

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for reports, our legitimate interest in a

platform without inadmissible pictures (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

  • Retention period: the role while you are a member of the crew; sharing setting and last active day until your

account is deleted; reports as in Section 22; nudges 15 days; everything is included in the data export

34. Reports about the vehicle catalogue

If you cannot find your car in the catalogue or something about an engine variant is wrong (for example the name,
technical data, rarity, Carpedia text or brand), you can report this to us in the app (“Model missing” or “Report an
error”). For this purpose, we store your account, the selected category, your text (at most 300 characters), the time
and the reference to the engine variant or to the place in the catalogue where you were searching. If you report a
missing model while assigning a sighting, we also store the reference to this sighting. If “Include photo” is switched
on (default: on), we look at the photo of this sighting for the review. It is not uploaded again for this purpose: it is
the photo already stored, on which the app searched for licence plates before uploading and, where found, obscured them
(Section 4). If you delete the sighting, its photo is no longer available for the report either. A report never
includes your location, coordinates, the time a photo was taken or place names. Please do not write any personal data
in the text. Only the operator sees reports, other users never do; we do not pass them on and do not tell you the
outcome.

  • Legal basis: performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a correct and

complete vehicle catalogue (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

  • Retention period: until the report has been dealt with; we delete resolved reports 180 days after they were resolved,

and all reports at the latest when your account is deleted; your reports are included in the data export
